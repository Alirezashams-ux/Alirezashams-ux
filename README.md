# Alireza Mahvelati Shamsabadi

### Trustworthy AI for Physical Infrastructure

PhD researcher at the University of Ulsan developing **interpretable, reliability-aware, and evidence-governed AI** for climate-resilient and safety-critical infrastructure.

My work focuses on a simple question:

> **How can AI systems remain useful, interpretable, and reliable when the physical system, climate regime, data distribution, and operator constraints change?**

I work across **water and wastewater systems, BESS, industrial infrastructure, and emerging energy/thermal systems**, combining machine learning with physical constraints, temporal validity, uncertainty, domain shift, and operational decision limits.

## Research Philosophy

I am interested in AI systems that do more than maximize predictive accuracy.

For physical infrastructure, a useful model should also answer:

- **Is the prediction temporally valid?**
- **Does the model remain reliable under regime or domain shift?**
- **Can the decision be explained operationally?**
- **Is the model aware of uncertainty and limited intervention capacity?**
- **Can the result be traced back to data, model version, and evidence?**

My long-term research direction is toward **trustworthy decision intelligence for physical infrastructure**: AI systems that integrate prediction, interpretation, reliability, physical constraints, provenance, and human oversight.
