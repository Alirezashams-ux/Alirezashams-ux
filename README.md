# Alireza Mahvelati Shamsabadi

### Trustworthy AI for Physical Infrastructure

PhD researcher at the University of Ulsan developing interpretable,
reliability-aware, and evidence-governed AI for climate-resilient and
safety-critical infrastructure.

> climate regimes, data distributions, and operational constraints change?
